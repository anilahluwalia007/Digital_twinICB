Integrated Dynamic BioProcessing (IDB) — mAb digital twin

N-1 perfusion seed → N reactor → TFF cell removal → surge tank → Brij-9 in-line → Protein A (columns 1&2, 3&4) → depth filtration → Tris base/NaCl in-line → AEX → acetic acid in-line → CEX → virus filtration → UF/DF → BDS hold → 0.2 µm → cryovault. Every step of every unit runs in sequence; solutions are tracked by name. Day axis = campaign day (N-1 first, then N, then the downstream run-out).
