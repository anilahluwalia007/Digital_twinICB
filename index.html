Integrated Dynamic BioProcessing (IDB) — mAb digital twin

N-1 perfusion seed → N reactor (feed in = cells + broth + product out) → TFF cell removal → harvest sump → Protein A → low-pH VI → CEX/AEX → virus filter → SPTFF. Buffer and wastewater follow the grams recovered each day, driven by the molecule's permeate titre curve. Day axis = campaign day (N-1 first, then N).
